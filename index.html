About the project

Context

Beekeeping still relies on manual and poorly standardized processes. Without reliable data and management tools, beekeepers face low predictability, productivity losses, and difficulty tracking hives, tasks, and teams. This makes management reactive, unsustainable, and vulnerable to unforeseen events.

Problem

Essential data such as temperature, humidity, production, and hive conditions is hard to record and track. Tasks, teams, and apiary visits don't follow a structured workflow, so beekeepers only discover a problem once it has already caused impact. Without indicators, productivity and hive welfare are compromised.

Challenge

Develop a digital solution capable of modernizing beekeeping management by bringing intelligent monitoring, data centralization, and organization to all stages, without adding complexity to the beekeeper's daily routine.

BBEE — Smart Apiculture is a web and mobile platform that brings together apiary management, intelligent monitoring, and operational organization. It centralizes data, tasks, location, and production, so beekeepers can act preventively, strategically, and sustainably.

Mobile app

Fieldwork is the heart of beekeeping management.

With clear processes, executing each planned action stops being a challenge and becomes part of an efficient workflow — even without connection.

  • Track and execute tasks even without connection.
  • Have a complete history of interventions and activities.
  • Teamwork in the apiary, with a clear and organized workflow.
  • Capture field information the moment it happens.

Solution flow

The customer's CPF is entered to access their profile and consult essential information, such as address and contracted plans. This data helps the salesperson understand the customer profile and make a more assertive offer.

Offers available according to the customer's credit score. On the contracting data screen, the customer provides the address that will be linked to the offer to check coverage availability.

After the address is provided, we analyze the available coverage and start configuring the offer. In this step, the lines that will be linked to the chosen plan are defined, including the mobile and landline voice lines the customer wants to use.

If the plan includes fiber, installation must be scheduled, with two date options for the technician's visit to the customer's home. Next, a summary screen is presented, where all information defined with the customer is reviewed before the sale is completed.

As a security step, the customer's identity is verified through biometrics before the sale is finalized. After identity validation, the sale is completed successfully.
